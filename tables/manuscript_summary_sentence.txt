Among a modeled cohort of patients with Lynch syndrome already undergoing surveillance colonoscopy, performing endometrial biopsy during the same sedated encounter cost an estimated $543.18 per patient, compared with $875.26 for standalone office biopsy and $4,101.64 for operative dilation and curettage. Adding EMB to colonoscopy cost $332.09 less than performing EMB separately (37.9%). The combined strategy remains the least expensive strategy as long as the incremental colonoscopy-suite time stays below approximately 14.9 minutes.
