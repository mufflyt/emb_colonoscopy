Among a modeled cohort of patients with Lynch syndrome already undergoing surveillance colonoscopy, performing endometrial biopsy during the same sedated encounter cost an estimated $553.45 per patient, compared with $914.38 for standalone office biopsy and $4,387.14 for operative dilation and curettage. Adding EMB to colonoscopy cost $360.92 less than performing EMB separately (39.5%). The combined strategy remains the least expensive strategy as long as the incremental colonoscopy-suite time stays below approximately 15.8 minutes.
