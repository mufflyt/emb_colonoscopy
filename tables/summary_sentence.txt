Among a modeled cohort of patients with Lynch syndrome already undergoing surveillance colonoscopy, performing endometrial biopsy during the same sedated encounter cost an estimated $499.19 per patient, compared with $707.89 for standalone office biopsy and $2,879.90 for operative dilation and curettage. Adding EMB to colonoscopy cost $208.69 less than performing EMB separately (29.5%). The combined strategy remains the least expensive strategy as long as the incremental colonoscopy-suite time stays below approximately 11.2 minutes.
