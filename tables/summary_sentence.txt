Among a modeled cohort of patients with Lynch syndrome already undergoing surveillance colonoscopy, performing endometrial biopsy during the same sedated encounter cost an estimated $486.42 per patient, compared with $727.48 for standalone office biopsy and $3,822.79 for operative dilation and curettage. Adding EMB to colonoscopy cost $241.06 less than performing EMB separately (33.1%). The combined strategy remains the least expensive strategy as long as the incremental colonoscopy-suite time stays below approximately 12.2 minutes.
