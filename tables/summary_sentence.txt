Among a modeled cohort of patients with Lynch syndrome already undergoing surveillance colonoscopy, performing endometrial biopsy during the same sedated encounter cost an estimated $574.77 per patient, compared with $764.93 for standalone office biopsy and $3,827.04 for operative dilation and curettage. Adding EMB to colonoscopy cost $190.16 less than performing EMB separately (24.9%). The combined strategy remains the least expensive strategy as long as the incremental colonoscopy-suite time stays below approximately 10.7 minutes.
