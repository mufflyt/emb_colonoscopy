Among a modeled cohort of patients with Lynch syndrome already undergoing surveillance colonoscopy, performing endometrial biopsy during the same sedated encounter cost an estimated $496.71 per patient, compared with $743.54 for standalone office biopsy and $3,849.49 for operative dilation and curettage. Adding EMB to colonoscopy cost $246.83 less than performing EMB separately (33.2%). The combined strategy remains the least expensive strategy as long as the incremental colonoscopy-suite time stays below approximately 12.4 minutes.
