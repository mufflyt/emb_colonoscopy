Among a modeled cohort of patients with Lynch syndrome already undergoing surveillance colonoscopy, performing endometrial biopsy during the same sedated encounter cost an estimated $486.01 per patient, compared with $780.23 for standalone office biopsy and $3,827.04 for operative dilation and curettage. Adding EMB to colonoscopy cost $294.22 less than performing EMB separately (37.7%). The combined strategy remains the least expensive strategy as long as the incremental colonoscopy-suite time stays below approximately 13.8 minutes.
