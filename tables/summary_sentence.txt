Among a modeled cohort of patients with Lynch syndrome already undergoing surveillance colonoscopy, performing endometrial biopsy during the same sedated encounter cost an estimated $530.37 per patient, compared with $816.40 for standalone office biopsy and $3,827.04 for operative dilation and curettage. Adding EMB to colonoscopy cost $286.03 less than performing EMB separately (35%). The combined strategy remains the least expensive strategy as long as the incremental colonoscopy-suite time stays below approximately 13.6 minutes.
