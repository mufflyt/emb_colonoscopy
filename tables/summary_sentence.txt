Among a modeled cohort of patients with Lynch syndrome already undergoing surveillance colonoscopy, performing endometrial biopsy during the same sedated encounter cost an estimated $506.11 per patient, compared with $749.18 for standalone office biopsy and $3,839.81 for operative dilation and curettage. Adding EMB to colonoscopy cost $243.06 less than performing EMB separately (32.4%). The combined strategy remains the least expensive strategy as long as the incremental colonoscopy-suite time stays below approximately 12.3 minutes.
